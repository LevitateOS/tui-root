      Rec Tooling          ▸       What is LevitateOS and why does it exist?
                                 A
                                   LevitateOS is an independent operating system for
                                   daily use, competing directly with Arch Linux. It
                                   borrows Arch's philosophy (you maintain your own
                                   packages on a minimal base). Its userland (glibc,
                                   systemd, coreutils, OpenSSH and the rest of a minimal
                                   base) is built from Fedora 45 RPM packages instead of
                                   being compiled, and it keeps two root filesystems, A
                                   and B: the booted one is locked and changes are made
                                   in the other.
